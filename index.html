The Method

Everything We Do Runs On S.O.S.

Three things separate the agent who wins the listing from the one who gets "we're going to think about it." We drill all three, every session, until they're reflex.

S — Scripts

Word-for-word language for the calls that actually move your business — seller appointments, buyer agreements, the listing presentation. Learned properly, then made yours.

O — Objections

One objection handler per class, dissected and rehearsed live. Commission, timing, price, "my brother-in-law is an agent" — you'll have an answer you believe in.

S — Skills

Delivery, tone, pacing, silence. The difference between reading a script and running a conversation. Built through individual scenarios and challenges in the room.

Every Class

The Same Four Parts, Every Session.

Consistency is the point. You always know what you're walking into — and you always leave having said the words out loud.

  1. 01

    Mindset

    We open by getting your head right. Skills don't survive a bad frame.

  2. 02

    Roleplay

    Live, in the room, with a partner. Not a demo you watch — a rep you take.

  3. 03

    Individual Scenarios

    Your challenges, your deals, your stuck conversations — worked one at a time.

  4. 04

    One Objection Handler

    A single objection, taken apart and rehearsed until it stops being scary.

Questions

Is any of this online?

No. Humpty Dumpty is in person by design — roleplay, feedback and reading a room don't transfer well through a screen. Your two monthly flash calls are by phone.

How much roleplay is there, really?

Every class. It's one of the four fixed segments, alongside individual scenarios where your own stuck conversations get worked in front of the group. If you don't want to speak out loud, this isn't the right program.

What exactly is a flash call?

A short, on-demand call before a high-stakes conversation — most often right before a listing appointment. You get two a month, included.

Do I have to follow the twelve topics in order?

The twelve are a showcase of what gets covered, not a fixed or exhaustive syllabus. Order moves with the market and what the room needs, and topics get added.

Is this for buyer agents or listing agents?

Both, and the strongest agents work both sides. The catalogue is deliberately split — some topics are seller-focused, some buyer-focused, several are neither.

Do I need to apply?

No. Every Fast Track Farming 101 graduate is already eligible — just register below to save your seat.

Is there an income requirement to join?

No. This is for agents who are motivated and serious about success, not a specific income level. Every Fast Track Farming 101 grad is welcome, whatever stage you're at.

I haven't done Fast Track Farming 101 — can I join?

Not yet. Humpty Dumpty is the continuing education track for Fast Track Farming 101 grads — complete that first, and you're automatically eligible here.
